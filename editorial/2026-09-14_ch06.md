# Chapter 6 revision / 第 6 章修訂

2026-09-14 · v0.6.0 · Revised working draft / 修訂工作稿

## Changes and proofs / 修正與證明

Rebuilt the foundations around a fixed finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space, unit-vector representatives, pure-state rays, general density states and specified measurement rules. Basis coordinates are amplitudes; their squared magnitudes become probabilities only for the corresponding measurement. A unique empirical encoding of social configurations is not assumed.

以固定有限維複 Hilbert 空間、單位向量代表、純態射線、一般密度態及指定測量規則重建基礎。基底座標是振幅，只有對應測量下的模平方才是機率；不假設社會配置具有唯一實證編碼。

Proposition 6.1 now requires both vectors to be normalized and distinguishes the two equality cases: maximal absolute overlap means the same ray, while zero means orthogonality. These conditions do not automatically identify social configurations.

命題 6.1 明定兩個向量均須歸一化，並區分兩種等號：最大絕對重疊表示同一射線，零重疊表示正交。這些條件不自動識別社會配置。

Corrected nonorthogonal superposition by retaining its complex cross term and normalizing only a nonzero sum. The exact distinct-vector counterexample has coefficient squared magnitudes summing to one but vector squared norm 197/125. The formation example uses 1/sqrt(2), 1/2, 1/2 instead of the first-edition rounded 0.71, 0.50, 0.50; the old squared norm is 1.0041.

非正交疊加保留複交叉項，且僅對非零向量和歸一化。精確相異向量反例的係數模平方和為一，向量範數平方卻為 197/125。陣型例子改用 1/sqrt(2)、1/2、1/2，取代第一版捨入的 0.71、0.50、0.50；原範數平方為 1.0041。

Proposition 6.2 supplies the positivity and trace proof for normalized ensembles, together with the converse spectral construction. Table 6.1 and the equal-ensemble example distinguish a coherent pure state from a mixture and demonstrate that one basis measurement does not identify a full density operator. Two measurement settings still leave an imaginary coherence unobserved.

命題 6.2 補上歸一化系綜的半正定性及跡證明，並提供反向譜建構。表 6.1 與等權系綜例子區分相干純態與混合，展示單一基底測量無法識別完整密度算子；即使使用本例的兩種測量設定，仍有虛部相干未被觀察。

Defined composite systems through an explicit tensor factorization, distinguished mixed-state separability from product structure and proved the reduced-state/local-measurement identity in coordinates. Non-product mixed states are not automatically entangled.

以明確張量因子分解定義複合系統，區分混態的可分性與乘積結構，並以座標證明約化態與局部測量等式。非乘積混態不自動具有纏結。

Recast observables as real-scored POVMs or Hermitian spectral measurements. Added a short proof that entropy cannot equal the expectation of one fixed single-copy operator for every state, using two zero-entropy pure states whose mixture has entropy one. Nonlinear estimators from repeated observations remain possible but are a separate statistical task.

可觀測量改為實數分數的 POVM 或 Hermitian 譜測量。以兩個熵為零的純態混合後熵為一，簡短證明熵不能對所有狀態都等於同一固定單份算子的期望。重複觀察下的非線性估計仍可能，但屬另外的統計工作。

Proposition 6.3 replaces vector distance on pure states with a phase-minimized ray metric and proves its closed form, definiteness and triangle inequality. A separate Hilbert–Schmidt metric covers density states; its pure-projector expression is derived. Neither metric is asserted to measure social reorganization cost.

命題 6.3 將純態上的向量距離改為相位最小化的射線度量，證明閉式、正定性與三角不等式。另用 Hilbert–Schmidt 度量涵蓋密度態，並推導純態投影表示式；不宣稱任一度量衡量社會重組成本。

Theorem 6.1 and Corollary 6.1 retain the valid normalization calculation with explicit finite-dimensional continuity, Hermiticity and normalized initial data. The generator K has inverse-time units, consistent with Chapter 4. Unitary density propagation is proved; Lindblad evolution is restricted to the stated continuous CPTP semigroup assumptions. A trace argument explicitly excludes exact bosonic commutation in this finite core.

定理 6.1 與推論 6.1 保留合法的歸一化計算，明定有限維連續性、Hermitian 條件與歸一化初值。生成元 K 採時間倒數單位，與第 4 章一致。證明酉密度傳播；Lindblad 演化限於所述連續 CPTP 半群假設。跡論證明確排除有限核心中的精確玻色對易關係。
