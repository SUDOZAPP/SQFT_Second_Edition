## Changes / 修改

Replaced broad historical and disciplinary necessity claims with explicit criteria for the usefulness of a mathematical language. Retained relational motivation without claiming that mathematical fields create biological people. Distinguished records, mathematical objects and social interpretation as mutually constraining parts of model construction.

以數學語言用途的明確判準，取代廣泛歷史與學科必要性主張。保留關係取向動機，不宣稱數學場創造生物學上的人。區分紀錄、數學物件與社會詮釋，說明三者在建模時彼此約束。

Definition 3.1 now requires a specified correspondence and preserved structure. Equation (3.1) supplies a concrete update-preservation condition; its limitations for non-injective mappings and observations are stated. Equation (3.2) connects the Chapter 1 measurement model to coded observations, without claiming identifiability or causation.

定義 3.1 要求明定對應及保留結構。式（3.1）給出具體更新保留條件，並說明非單射映射與觀測方面的限制。式（3.2）連接第 1 章測量模型與編碼觀察，不宣稱可識別性或因果性。

The original Proposition 3.1 (Mathematical Independence) is reclassified as methodological principle 3.A. The new Proposition 3.1 proves invariance of outcome probabilities under simultaneous unitary conjugation of states and measurement effects. The full proof remains in the main text, including positivity, normalization and POVM completeness. Example 3.1 gives an exact two-dimensional calculation and contrasts transforming the state alone. These are standard mathematical facts, not new physical principles or empirical results.

原命題 3.1（數學獨立性）改列方法論原則 3.A。新命題 3.1 證明狀態與測量效應同時酉共軛時，結果機率不變。完整證明留在正文，包含正半定性、正規化及 POVM 完備性。例 3.1 提供精確二維計算，並對比僅變換狀態的結果。這些屬標準數學事實，不是新的物理原理或實證結果。後續交叉引用須區分新舊命題。
