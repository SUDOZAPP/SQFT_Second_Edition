# Chapter 4 revision / 第 4 章修訂

2026-09-12 · v0.4.0 · Revised draft / 已修訂草稿

## Principal changes / 主要修正

Reconstructed the eight-part framework as a finite-dimensional core with explicit mathematical conditions and interpretive commitments. It no longer treats local social roles as universally created by field operators, non-product states as automatically entangled, or contextual capital as independent of actor-specific holdings. Product and classically correlated states remain admissible.

將八項框架重建為具有明確數學條件與詮釋承諾的有限維核心。不再把社會角色普遍視為場算子產物、把非乘積態自動視為糾纏，或把情境資本寫成不涉及行動者資源差異的量。允許乘積態及經典關聯態。

Replaced unnormalized projection with an instrument-based update and normalized positive-probability conditioning. Proposition 4.1 supplies the full positivity and trace proof and explicitly excludes zero-probability conditioning. Unconditional updates are distinguished from conditioning on a retained outcome.

以儀器更新及正機率條件下的正規化，修正未正規化投影。命題 4.1 附完整正性與跡的證明，明確排除零機率條件更新。區分保留結果的條件更新與非條件更新。

Topology becomes a declared descriptor module rather than an automatic property of a density matrix. CPTP dynamics are stated as a model restriction. Lindblad form requires an additional norm-continuous, time-homogeneous semigroup assumption, rather than following from openness. The frequency generator K has inverse-time units; later energy-like conventions must supply their conversion. No universal social Planck constant is introduced.

拓撲成為須明定的描述模組，不視為密度矩陣的自動性質。CPTP 動力學列為模型限制；Lindblad 形式需要額外範數連續、時間齊次半群假設，並非由開放性推出。頻率生成元 K 以時間倒數為單位，後續類能量約定須提供換算，不引入普遍社會 Planck 常數。

Proposition 4.2 constructs a four-dimensional model with a separable correlated initial state, projective instruments, a declared score, an optional fixed graph and a depolarizing semigroup. The proof exhibits sixteen Kraus operators and verifies composition and the generator. It demonstrates compatibility of this restricted core, not the consistency of every later extension or empirical adequacy.

命題 4.2 建構四維模型，包括可分但相關的初態、投影儀器、指定評分、選用固定圖與去極化半群。證明列出十六個 Kraus 算子，驗證組合及生成元；展示此限定核心的相容性，不認證所有後續擴充或經驗適切性。
