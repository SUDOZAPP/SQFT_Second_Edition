# Chapter 1 revision / 第 1 章修訂

Date: 2026-09-09 · Version: 0.1.0 · Status: revised draft / 已修訂草稿。

## Changes / 修訂內容

- Reframed SQFT as a model-building research programme; removed unsupported universal claims about other approaches.
  將 SQFT 定位為模型建構研究計畫，移除對其他進路缺乏支持的全稱判斷。
- Preserved the relational motivation and clarified that the matrix formalism is a modelling choice rather than a deduction from Bourdieu.
  保留關係取向動機，明確區分布迪厄的概念背景與矩陣模型的選擇。
- Added finite-dimensional states, a specified observation rule, Proposition 1.1 with a complete proof, and a derivation of the classical diagonal special case.
  新增有限維狀態、觀測規則、命題 1.1 的完整證明與經典對角特例推導。
- Distinguished open evolution, Markov semigroups, spatial fields and operator-valued fields; separated mathematical validity from empirical and causal claims.
  區分開放演化、Markov 半群、空間場與算子值場，分清數學合法性與經驗、因果主張。
- Added four chapter-specific references and restored eight individually named introductory sections.
  新增四筆本章文獻，將導論整理為八個獨立小節。

## Checks / 查核

The probability proof uses positive semidefiniteness and trace cyclicity; the classical reduction follows by multiplication of diagonal matrices. These are standard results, explicitly identified as such. The English and Traditional Chinese text has been paired and checked for corresponding assumptions and conclusions. Complete-book KaTeX compilation reports no errors; browser validation is recorded separately.

機率命題使用正半定性與跡的循環性；經典特例由對角矩陣乘法得到。兩者均明示為標準結果。英文與繁體中文逐段對照，假設與結論相符；全書 KaTeX 編譯無錯誤，瀏覽器驗證另存。
