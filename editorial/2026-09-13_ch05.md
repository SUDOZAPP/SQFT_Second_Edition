# Chapter 5 revision / 第 5 章修訂

2026-09-13 · v0.5.0 · Revised draft / 已修訂草稿

## Principal changes / 主要修正

Retitled Chapter 5 “A Worked Model of Relational Coordination in Football” and synchronized its table-of-contents entry. Preserved the first-edition phrase in a historical opening sentence. Replaced the unspecified field/entanglement example with a fully specified binary shared-cue model: a fair cue, independent symmetric noise, joint probabilities, a diagonal density state, a directional alignment score and an independent baseline.

第 5 章改題為「足球關係協調的可計算模型」，同步目錄標題；第一版用語保留在開場的歷史說明。以完整指定的二元共同線索模型，取代原先未明定的場／纏結例子，包括公平線索、獨立對稱雜訊、聯合機率、對角密度態、方向一致分數與獨立基準。

Separated dependence, non-product states, entanglement, predictive information and tactical benefit. The explicit product-state mixture proves that this model is separable. Its marginals are identical at every noise level although the joint score varies. Mutual information is derived in bits and is not presented as an entanglement witness or causal benefit measure.

區分相依性、非乘積態、纏結、預測資訊與戰術效益。明示的乘積態混合證明本模型可分；各雜訊水準的邊際相同，聯合分數卻會改變。互資訊以位元推導，不視為纏結見證或因果效益量。

Corrected the projection update by dividing by the positive event probability. The example gives both the conditional trace-one state and the unchanged nonselective diagonal state. Explained why conditioning on a recorded direction is not a physical-collapse claim.

投影更新補上正事件機率作為分母；例子同時給出跡為一的條件態，以及不保留結果時不變的對角態。說明依紀錄方向取條件並非物理塌縮主張。

The original Proposition 5.1 inferred factorization from locally optimal action without defining a payoff or information set. Removed that invalid proof. New Proposition 5.1 derives the joint law and alignment gain; new Proposition 5.2 proves the best binary prediction accuracy with and without an informative record under zero–one loss. Its proof handles all deterministic choices and independent randomization. It is explicitly not an intervention or optimal football policy theorem.

原命題 5.1 未定義報酬與資訊集合，便從局部最優行動推出因子化，該證明無效，已撤換。新命題 5.1 推導聯合分布與方向一致增益；新命題 5.2 在零一損失下證明有、無相關紀錄資訊時的最佳二元預測準確率，涵蓋確定性選擇與獨立隨機化。它明確不是介入或最優足球政策定理。

Coaching Hamiltonians and tactical manifolds are now identified as possible extensions requiring mathematical specification and empirical justification. The state family is algebraically matched to Chapter 4's depolarizing example without claiming that the parameter follows a real-time fatigue or coaching law. Data requirements and the inability of fixed-basis records to identify off-diagonal density entries are stated.

教練哈密頓量與戰術流形改列為需要數學指定及實證支持的可能擴充。狀態族在代數上對應第 4 章去極化例子，但不主張參數遵循真實時間的疲勞或教練法則。補充資料需求，以及固定基底紀錄無法識別密度矩陣非對角元素的限制。
