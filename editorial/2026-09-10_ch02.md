## Corrections / 修正

- Distinguished the sociological field from a mathematical field; replaced the automatic identity F=(V,E,W) with a typed, explicitly selective graph representation.
  區分社會學場域與數學場；將直接等同的 F=(V,E,W) 改為定義明確、表明選擇性範圍的圖表示。
- Replaced C=C(F) as the principal capital formula with an actor-, category- and context-specific operational score. The degree illustration from the long volume is retained conditionally: context change need not cause a nonzero score change.
  主要資本公式改為包含行動者、類別與情境的操作性評分。保留長卷的學位案例，但改為條件式說明：情境變化不保證評分差值非零。
- Removed the inference that similar habitus entails synchronization; any such claim requires a dynamic model and a definition.
  移除相似慣習即蘊含同步的推論；同步需要動力學模型與定義。
- Added Proposition 2.1 with a complete elementary proof: conditional independence given a shared variable can coexist with marginal dependence. Coordination alone therefore does not demonstrate the necessity of a quantum model.
  新增命題 2.1 及完整初等證明：給定共同變數的條件獨立，可與邊際相依並存；協調本身無法證明量子模型的必要性。
- The original Proposition 2.1 (Field Primacy) is now modelling principle 2.A. It was already qualified as methodological in the longer edition; this qualification is preserved and strengthened. The new proposition is a different mathematical statement. Future cross-references must distinguish them.
  原命題 2.1（場優先）改列建模原則 2.A。長版原文已限定其方法論地位，此限定獲保留並強化；新命題是另一數學敘述，後續交互引用須加以區分。
- Replaced the graph-versus-field dichotomy with a field defined on graph nodes and acknowledged higher-order alternatives. Added operational, causal and model-comparison requirements; corrected the transition to philosophy in Chapter 3 and axioms in Chapter 4.
  以圖節點上的場取代圖與場的二分，納入高階替代表示；補充操作化、因果與模型比較要求，修正第 3 章哲學、第 4 章公理的銜接。
- Organized the chapter into eleven individually named bilingual subsections with two chapter-specific references.
  整理為十一個獨立命名的英中小節，新增兩筆本章文獻。
