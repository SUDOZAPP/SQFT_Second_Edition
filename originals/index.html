Editorial Retrospective 編輯回顧

From Manuscript to Research Program從手稿到研究計畫

The earliest conception of Social Quantum Field Theory (SQFT) was not intended to establish a finished theory. Rather, it began with a modest mathematical question: Can the language of fields, symmetries, geometry, and variational principles be generalized to describe abstract relational systems beyond their traditional physical domains?

社會量子場論（SQFT）的最初構想，並非旨在建立一套完成的理論。相反，它始於一個謙遜的數學問題：場、對稱、幾何與變分原理的語言，能否被推廣以描述超越其傳統物理領域的抽象關係系統？

As the project evolved, it became apparent that answering this question required more than introducing new notation. It demanded a coherent mathematical architecture capable of integrating concepts from multiple disciplines while preserving internal consistency and maintaining clear distinctions between formal mathematics and empirical interpretation. This realization transformed the project from a single manuscript into a broader research program.

隨著計畫演進，顯然回答這個問題需要的不只是引入新符號。它要求一套連貫的數學架構，能夠整合多學科概念，同時保持內部一致性，並清楚區分形式數學與經驗詮釋。此一體認使計畫從單一手稿轉變為更廣泛的研究計畫。

Chapter 1 · Introduction 第 1 章 · 導論

1.1 The Motivation 1.1 動機

Modern science has achieved remarkable success in describing the physical universe through mathematically precise theories. Classical mechanics explains planetary motion, quantum mechanics describes microscopic systems, and quantum field theory unifies particles and interactions within a common mathematical language.

現代科學透過數學上精確的理論，在描述物理宇宙方面取得了卓越成就。古典力學解釋行星運動，量子力學描述微觀系統，量子場論則在共同的數學語言中統一粒子與相互作用。

By contrast, the mathematical description of complex social systems remains comparatively fragmented. Economics, sociology, political science, and network theory each provide valuable perspectives, yet no widely accepted mathematical framework exists for representing relational structures as fundamental dynamical objects.

6.1 Social State Space 6.1 社會狀態空間

社會狀態依 $i\hbar \frac{d}{dt}|\Psi(t)\rangle = H|\Psi(t)\rangle$ 演化。對開放系統可恢復 Lindblad 形式。定理 6.1（歸一化守恆）：若 $H = H^\dagger$，則 $\frac{d}{dt}\langle\Psi|\Psi\rangle = 0$。

P.1 Why Another Mathematical Framework? P.1 為何需要另一個數學框架？

Graph theory, dynamical systems, statistical mechanics, network science, information theory, and machine learning already provide powerful tools. The motivation behind SQFT is not that these frameworks are insufficient individually, but that they often emphasize different aspects of the same relational phenomena. SQFT seeks a common mathematical language within which connectivity, dynamics, uncertainty, structure, invariance, and relationships among structures may coexist and interact.

圖論、動力系統、統計力學、網絡科學、資訊理論與機器學習已提供強大工具。SQFT 的動機並非這些框架各自不足，而是它們往往強調同一關係現象的不同面向。SQFT 試圖提供共同數學語言，使連通性、動力學、不確定性、結構、不變性與結構之間的關係得以共存並互動。

P.2 The Role of Analogy P.2 類比的角色

Throughout this monograph, concepts originating in theoretical physics—fields, gauge symmetry, renormalization, topology—have been adopted as mathematical structures rather than as literal descriptions of social reality. The analogy is deliberate and limited: it supplies precise language and proven organizational principles, not ontological claims about microscopic social processes.

全書中，源自理論物理的概念——場、規範對稱、重整化、拓撲——被採納為數學結構，而非對社會實在的字面描述。此類比是刻意且有限度的：它提供精確語言與已驗證的組織原則，而非關於微觀社會過程的本體論主張。

Social Quantum Field Theory should not be understood merely as the application of concepts from quantum field theory to the social sciences. Its central objective is to investigate whether a broad class of complex relational systems can be described using a common mathematical architecture built upon variational principles, field representations, symmetry, topology, information geometry, operator methods, and category theory.

社會量子場論不應僅被理解為將量子場論概念應用於社會科學。其核心目標是探究：廣泛的複雜關係系統，是否能以變分原理、場表示、對稱、拓撲、資訊幾何、算子方法與範疇論所構成的共同數學架構加以描述。

2. Structural Correspondences 2. 結構對應
