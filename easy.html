CHAPTER 1 / 第 1 章

為什麼要一起看人與關係Why relationships matter

依已修訂草稿解說 / Based on revised draft

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 1 · Introduction 第 1 章 · 導論

這章在問什麼 / The question

This book asks how shared rules and relationships shape what people can do. SQFT proposes a mathematical description of these patterns, then asks whether it helps explain observations. A model is a selective description: it must say what it measures and how it could be wrong.

本書想問：共同規則與人際關係，如何影響每個人能做什麼？SQFT 嘗試用數學描述這些模式，再檢查是否有助於解釋觀察。模型是一種有選擇的描述，必須說清楚測量什麼，以及什麼結果會顯示它有問題。

生活例子 / An everyday example

Imagine a school project. Knowing each student's ability helps, but deadlines, division of work and shared information also affect the result. The model tries to include those relationships, rather than treating the team score as a simple sum of individual abilities.

想像一份小組作業。知道每個同學的能力很有用，但期限、分工和資訊共享也會影響成果。模型想把這些關係納入，而不只是把個人能力加總成團隊分數。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

The revised chapter supplies a valid probability rule under specified mathematical assumptions. That establishes how the model calculates; it does not establish that the model describes every team correctly.

已修訂正文在指定的數學假設下，給出合法的機率計算規則。這說明模型如何計算，尚不表示它能正確描述每個團隊。

CHAPTER 2 / 第 2 章

同一份資源，在不同環境有不同作用A setting changes what a resource can do

依已修訂草稿解說 / Based on revised draft

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 2 · From Bourdieu’s Field Theory to Social Quantum Field Theory 第 2 章 · 從布迪厄場論到社會量子場論

這章在問什麼 / The question

The chapter connects SQFT to Bourdieu's attention to social positions, resources and acquired habits. It distinguishes a sociological field—a structured setting of positions and stakes—from a mathematical field, which assigns values across a defined domain.

本章連結布迪厄對社會位置、資源與後天形成習慣的關注。社會學的「場域」是具有位置與利害關係的環境；數學的「場」則是在明確範圍內指派數值的工具，兩者需要另外建立對應。

生活例子 / An everyday example

A qualification may carry different weight when applying to a laboratory or an arts organization. Likewise, two players can coordinate because they learned the same play. A shared background can create a connection between their actions without a new message passing between them at that moment.

同一項資格，在申請實驗室或藝術機構時，受到的重視可能不同。同樣地，兩位球員可能因學過同一套戰術而配合；共同背景可以讓行動有關聯，不必在那一刻才傳遞新訊息。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

The revised chapter proves that shared context can create ordinary statistical dependence. Coordination alone is therefore insufficient evidence that a quantum description is necessary.

修訂章已證明，共同情境能產生一般的統計相依。因此，僅有協調現象，還不足以證明必須使用量子描述。

CHAPTER 3 / 第 3 章

換一種描述，預測可以不變Changing a description without changing predictions

依已修訂草稿解說 / Based on revised draft

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 3 · The Mathematical Philosophy of Social Quantum Field Theory 第 3 章 · 社會量子場論的數學哲學

這章在問什麼 / The question

The revised chapter distinguishes a mathematical model, the records it predicts and the social meaning assigned to its variables. A formal analogy needs a stated mapping and a statement of what it preserves. Sharing a word is not enough.

修訂章區分數學模型、模型預測的紀錄，以及變數被賦予的社會意義。形式類比需要明確映射，並交代保留什麼；只共用一個詞並不足夠。

生活例子 / An everyday example

Imagine changing the coordinate labels in both a table and its reading instructions. If both are changed consistently, the reported result can stay the same. The chapter proves a precise matrix version: transforming the state and measurement together preserves outcome probabilities.

想像同時改變表格的座標標記與閱讀說明。如果兩者一致地更改，報告結果可以不變。本章證明精確的矩陣版本：狀態與測量一起變換，會保留結果機率。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

Changing the state alone can change the answer. The proof establishes a specified equivalence of representations, not a cause of social behavior. The earlier philosophical proposition is now explicitly a methodological principle.

只改變狀態，答案可能改變。證明建立的是指定表示間的等價，而非社會行為的原因。原先的哲學命題，現在明確列為方法論原則。

CHAPTER 4 / 第 4 章

讓起點規則能一起成立Make the starting rules work together

依已修訂草稿解說 / Based on revised draft

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 4 · The Eight Axioms of Social Quantum Field Theory 第 4 章 · 社會量子場論的八公理

這章在問什麼 / The question

The revised chapter turns eight broad themes into explicit modelling requirements. It specifies valid states, joint descriptions, measurements and updates, while separating optional topology and memory-free dynamics from the basic model. A concrete four-state example shows that these restricted mathematical requirements can coexist.

修訂章把八項廣泛主題改成明確建模要求，指定合法狀態、共同描述、測量及更新，並把選用的拓撲與無記憶動力學和基本模型分開。一個四狀態例子展示：這些限定後的數學要求可以並存。

生活例子 / An everyday example

Suppose half the possible cases have a recorded outcome. If we keep only those cases, their probabilities must be rescaled to sum to one within that group. Keeping the original half-sized total would not describe the new conditional distribution. The chapter proves the corresponding matrix update and provides executable exact checks.

假設一半的可能案例具有某項記錄結果。若只留下這些案例，必須重新調整機率，讓它們在留下的群體內合計為一。沿用原本只有一半的總量，不能描述新的條件分配。本章證明相應矩陣更新，並提供可執行的精確檢查。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

A mathematically compatible toy model is not evidence that every organization follows it. Dependence need not be quantum entanglement; an open system need not have memory-free dynamics. The complete program is supplied as algebraic supplementary material, not as an empirical experiment.

數學上相容的玩具模型，不是每個組織都遵循它的證據。相依未必是量子糾纏，開放系統也未必沒有記憶。完整程式作為代數補充資料提供，不冒充實證實驗。

CHAPTER 5 / 第 5 章

共同線索如何幫助兩位球員配合How shared cues help two players align

依已修訂草稿解說 / Based on revised draft

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 5 · A Worked Model of Relational Coordination in Football 第 5 章 · 足球關係協調的可計算模型

這章在問什麼 / The question

The revised football example gives both players the same left-or-right cue, with independent chances of reversing it. This ordinary probability model lets us calculate how often their recorded directions match. The model can be written as a density matrix, but it remains classically correlated and has no entanglement.

修訂後的足球例子讓兩位球員收到相同的左或右線索，各自有獨立機會將它反轉。這個一般機率模型能計算兩人的紀錄方向有多常一致。模型雖可寫成密度矩陣，仍是古典相關，沒有纏結。

生活例子 / An everyday example

Imagine two teammates following a rehearsed cue. If each independently reverses it one quarter of the time, their directions match five eighths of the time. Two independent fair left-or-right choices match only half the time. The chapter derives these numbers, draws the assumed mechanism and supplies a complete checking program.

想像兩位隊友依循練習過的線索，各自獨立地有四分之一機會將它反轉。他們的方向會有八分之五的機率一致；若兩人各自公平且獨立地選左或右，一致機率只有二分之一。正文推導這些數字、繪出假設機制，並附上完整檢查程式。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

Matching direction is not the same as completing a pass or scoring. These are calculated examples, not measured football results. Knowing one recorded direction can improve prediction of the other in this model; this does not prove the best real-world passing strategy or a coaching effect.

方向一致不等於傳球成功或進球。這些是計算例子，不是實測足球結果。在此模型中，知道一個紀錄方向能改善對另一個方向的預測；但這不證明真實比賽的最優傳球策略或教練效果。

CHAPTER 6 / 第 6 章

表示可能性，不把表示誤當成觀察Representing possibilities without confusing them with observations

依已修訂草稿解說 / Based on revised draft

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 6 · Social Hilbert Space and the Geometry of Relational Possibility 第 6 章 · 社會希爾伯特空間與關係可能性的幾何

這章在問什麼 / The question

This chapter sets rules for using vectors and matrices to describe social possibilities. A model may describe one specific state or a probability mixture of several states. A measurement specifies how that description leads to recorded outcomes. The revision corrects how these descriptions are combined and compared.

本章訂定用向量與矩陣描述社會可能性的規則。模型可以描述一個特定狀態，或幾個狀態的機率混合；測量則指定這份描述如何連到紀錄結果。本次修訂更正這些描述的組合與比較方式。

生活例子 / An everyday example

A team might consider three formations with probabilities one half, one quarter and one quarter. Those observed frequencies alone do not tell us whether a single vector or an ordinary mixture is the better model. A worked table shows how different states can share the same first measurement statistics but differ under another explicitly defined measurement.

球隊可能以二分之一、四分之一與四分之一的機率考慮三種陣型。僅憑這些觀察頻率，無法判斷單一向量或一般混合，哪個模型較適合。正文的計算表展示：不同狀態在第一種測量下統計相同，在另一種明確定義的測量下卻可能不同。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

These are mathematical examples, not measurements on an actual team. A distance between model states is not automatically a social cost. Entropy, a summary of uncertainty, needs more care than assigning a score to one recorded outcome. Some later operator rules also require a different mathematical space and cannot simply be copied into finite matrices.

這些是數學例子，不是真實球隊的測量。模型狀態的距離不自動等於社會成本。熵是不確定性的摘要量，其處理比替單一紀錄結果打分更複雜。某些後續算子規則也需要另一種數學空間，不能直接套入有限矩陣。

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 7 · Social Field Operators and Local Excitations 第 7 章 · 社會場算子與局域激發

這章在問什麼 / The question

An operator is a mathematical rule that acts on a description. The chapter uses operations for adding, removing and counting modelled activities or roles, and for describing their interactions. These are tools for manipulating states, not explanations of how people come into existence.

算子是作用在描述上的數學規則。本章以運算表示模型中活動或角色的增加、移除與計數，也描述它們的相互作用。這是處理狀態的工具，不是在解釋人如何被創造出來。

生活例子 / An everyday example

An event system can register a new committee role or close an old one. The person may stay the same while their recorded role changes. This is an accessible picture of a local change relative to a background arrangement.

活動管理系統可以登記新的委員角色，也可以結束舊角色。人可能沒有變，但登記的角色變了。這可以幫助理解：某個局部相對於背景安排發生變化。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

The example does not justify the particular creation and annihilation algebra used in the manuscript. That algebra requires a specified state space and a reason why its rules fit the proposed application.

這個例子並未證成正文採用的特定產生與湮滅代數。該代數需要指定狀態空間，並說明其規則為何適合所提應用。

CHAPTER 8 / 第 8 章

連接方式與形狀Connections and shapes

正文待審訂；本章為解說 / Academic chapter pending review

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 8 · The Social Metric Tensor and Field Geometry 第 8 章 · 社會度量張量與場幾何

這章在問什麼 / The question

Although its title mentions a metric tensor, much of this chapter discusses topology: which parts are connected and which patterns survive allowed deformations. It also introduces indicators of collective order and asks what kind of change would alter a structural class.

雖然章名提到度量張量，正文很大部分在談拓撲：哪些部分相連，以及哪些模式在允許的變形下保留。它也引入集體秩序指標，詢問何種變化會改變結構類別。

生活例子 / An everyday example

Stretch a drawn loop without cutting it: the outline changes while it remains a loop. For an organization, a similarly motivated question is whether changing office locations also changes who can communicate with whom.

把畫出的圓環拉長而不剪斷，外形改了，但仍是環。對組織可以提出類似問題：改變辦公室位置，是否也改變了誰能與誰聯繫？

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

The analogy depends on what counts as a permitted change. Keeping one topological quantity fixed does not guarantee that an organization keeps its performance, fairness or practical stability.

類比取決於哪些變化被允許。一個拓撲量不變，不保證組織的表現、公平程度或實際穩定性也不變。

CHAPTER 9 / 第 9 章

拉遠看，描述也要改變Zooming out changes the description

正文待審訂；本章為解說 / Academic chapter pending review

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 9 · Renormalization and Multi-Scale Dynamics of Social Fields 第 9 章 · 社會場的重整化與多尺度動力學

這章在問什麼 / The question

Coarse-graining replaces fine detail with larger-scale summaries. Renormalization asks how the model's parameters should change when that scale changes. The aim is to keep useful predictions while deciding which details can be left out.

粗粒化是把細節換成較大尺度的摘要。重整化則追問：觀察尺度改變時，模型參數應如何跟著改？目標是在省略部分細節後，仍保有有用預測。

生活例子 / An everyday example

A minute-by-minute attendance record and a school's monthly attendance rate answer different questions. A relationship visible between two pupils need not retain the same strength when comparing whole classes.

逐分鐘出席紀錄與學校每月出席率回答不同問題。兩名學生之間可見的關係，在改成比較整個班級時，強度未必相同。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

A fixed point here concerns repeated changes of description scale. It is not automatically an institution that stays unchanged over calendar time; those are different forms of stability.

此處的固定點涉及反覆改變描述尺度，不自動等於一個隨日曆時間維持不變的制度；這是兩種不同的穩定概念。

對應正文 / Academic title: Chapter 10 · Gauge Symmetry and Conservation Laws in Social Fields 第 10 章 · 社會場中的規範對稱與守恆定律

這章在問什麼 / The question

Gauge symmetry asks which changes in mathematical representation leave the quantities of interest unchanged. The chapter proposes connections that allow local descriptions to be compared and discusses the relation between symmetry and conservation.

規範對稱關注：數學表示如何改變，而關心的量仍不變？本章提出讓不同局部描述互相比較的連接規則，也討論對稱與守恆的關係。

生活例子 / An everyday example

Two departments may label equivalent roles differently. A consistent translation should preserve the relationship being recorded. This offers an intuition for separating a choice of notation from a substantive change.

兩個部門可能用不同名稱標示相當的角色。一套一致的轉譯，應保留所記錄的關係。這有助於區分記號選擇與實質改變。

理解時要注意 / How far the explanation goes

Relabelling is only an introductory analogy. Mathematical gauge symmetry needs specified transformations and observables; it does not show that legitimacy, identity or symbolic capital is conserved.

重新命名只是入門類比。數學規範對稱需要指定變換與觀測量，並不能據此宣稱正當性、身分或象徵資本守恆。
